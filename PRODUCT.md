# Sous

## Register

brand

## Users

Sous is for independent French restaurateurs who are busy, French-speaking, and often non-technical. They need their public site, in-room QR menu, and price cards to stay aligned with the menu in their point-of-sale system. Multi-location restaurant groups are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Sous creates and maintains distinctive restaurant websites from one source of truth. A restaurateur can request an update by text or voice, review the draft, and explicitly approve it before the website and QR menu change. Success means the restaurant stays current without feeling templated or losing control of legally sensitive menu information.

## Brand Personality

Warm, assured, and appetising. The voice is French, calm, concise, and lightly playful. It should feel like a reliable, characterful restaurant rather than a generic software product.

## Anti-references

- Generic template-generator landing pages and interchangeable restaurant mockups.
- SaaS metric heroes, purple gradients, decorative glassmorphism, and repeated icon-card grids.
- Quiet gallery-editorial styling that makes the food and restaurant personality feel distant.
- Hype, silent automation, and claims that imply publishing without human approval.

## Design Principles

- Show that the menu is the single source of truth.
- Keep human validation visible wherever the product workflow is demonstrated.
- Let every restaurant example retain a distinct visual identity.
- Treat French menu compliance as a clear product feature, not legal fine print.
- Prefer working, explorable examples over decorative browser furniture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard-visible focus, comfortable touch targets, meaningful alternative text, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Maintain readable contrast and avoid relying on color alone for state or meaning.
